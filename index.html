About Thai Patio

Thai Patio is a Thai restaurant on N. Power Rd in Mesa, open seven days a week from 11:00am to 9:00pm. The kitchen cooks curries, noodles, fried rice, stir-fries and house specials to order, and every dish is made to your spice level.

  • All selections made to your spice level: mild, medium, hot or Thai hot
  • Lunch special every day, 11:00am – 2:30pm, served with steamed rice and an egg roll
  • Dine in, take out, or order online for pickup
  • Kitchen closes at 8:30pm
